# Retrospective Checklist (Example 2 – Hardware Verification Team)

## 1. What Went Well
- Power consumption tests completed successfully within design limits.  
- Signal integrity validation showed stable performance across all tested interfaces.  
- Lab coordination improved — equipment scheduling was efficient.  
- Firmware boot sequence reliability confirmed across multiple boards.

## 2. What Didn’t Go Well
- Thermal chamber calibration delayed stress testing by 2 days.  
- Limited prototype boards slowed parallel testing.  
- Defect logging was inconsistent — severity tags were not applied uniformly.  
- Communication gaps between firmware and hardware teams caused minor delays.

## 3. Improvements
- Establish preventive maintenance schedule for lab equipment.  
- Request additional prototype boards earlier in the project cycle.  
- Adopt standardized defect severity tagging system across the team.  
- Schedule weekly sync between firmware and hardware engineers.
