# Retrospective Checklist (Example 1 – Android Web Application Team)

## 1. What Went Well
- Guided tour feature was delivered on time and demo feedback was positive.  
- Google login integration passed QA without major defects.  
- Collaboration between frontend and backend improved — fewer handoff delays.  
- Sprint ceremonies (standups, planning) were well attended and efficient.

## 2. What Didn’t Go Well
- Analytics SDK integration took longer due to compatibility issues.  
- Mid-sprint tasks were modified, leading to unclear acceptance criteria.  
- QA team received late builds, reducing effective testing time.  

## 3. Improvements
- Ensure sprint tasks are locked after planning to avoid mid-sprint changes.
- Improve documentation of acceptance criteria in JIRA tickets.
- Create compatibility checklist for third-party SDKs before integration.  
- Ensure builds are delivered to QA at least 2 days before sprint end.
